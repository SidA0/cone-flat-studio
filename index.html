SHEET METAL / DEVELOPMENT WORKSPACE

From cone to flat blank.

Read analytic cone faces from a STEP model. Develop the surface into a true-size annular sector, then send STEP or DXF profiles to your laser shop.

HOW IT WORKS
3D cone → 2D sector

Preserves slant length and circumference along the developed surface.

i
Manufacturing check

Patterns describe the selected analytic cone surface. Confirm material thickness, seam allowance, kerf, bend and weld process, and any trim at the ends with your fabricator before cutting. STEP exports are planar sheet faces with zero thickness; DXF exports are cut contours.

    STEP sheet faces

    One analytic, planar, zero-thickness sheet face per blank. The ZIP contains a separate STEP file for each selected pattern.

    ▱

    DXF cut profiles

    One R12 DXF per blank, with exact ARC and LINE entities on the CUT layer. The ZIP keeps the files separate.

    ≡

    Dimension report

    CSV with 3D radii, cone half-angle, slant width, flat radii, sector sweep, and source face IDs.

    Files contain no kerf offset, weld overlap, tabs, or bend compensation. Add those in your manufacturing workflow.
